### [2] [The meaning of Milky Way mass estimates in the presence of the LMC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21732)
*Leandro Beraldo e Silva,Gurtina Besla,Nicolás Garavito-Camargo,Monica Valluri,Kathryne J. Daniel,Ekta Patel,Sergey E. Koposov,Laura L. Watkins,Amanda Byström,Alis J. Deason,Chervin F. P. Laporte*

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LMC近期到达扰动了银河系恒星晕的观测运动学，使基于平衡态假设的银心距 $r\gtrsim30$ kpc 质量估计产生偏差；本文不回避这一非平衡态，而是试图主动利用它同时称量MW与LMC。

Abstract: The LMC's recent arrival perturbs the observed kinematics of the Milky Way's (MW) stellar halo, complicating equilibrium-based MW mass estimates at Galactocentric distances $r\gtrsim 30$ kpc. Tracers in regions crossed by the LMC longest ago (Galactic coordinates $\ell >0^\circ, b>0^\circ$) are approximately relaxed and trace the total MW+LMC mass. Since the reflex motion is primarily latitudinal, a radial-velocity-only estimator ($M_{v_r}$) recovers the total MW+LMC mass with $\lesssim 5\%$ errors across $30<r/{\rm kpc}<200$, without any reflex-motion correction. Rather than treating the LMC-induced disequilibrium as an obstacle, our framework exploits it to weigh both galaxies.

RBH-1, discovered with HST and confirmed by JWST, provides the strongest evidence yet for a runaway SMBH: a bow shock and linear trail in the circumgalactic medium (CGM) of a $z=0.96$ galaxy. Runaway SMBH trails therefore offer a new probe of turbulence in the diffuse CGM, in regimes where its kinematics are otherwise inaccessible.
