### [1] [The birth and fate of stellar clumps in super-early galaxies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26993)
*B. Das,A. Ferrara,A. Pallottini,E. Ntormousi,M. Kohandel*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 通过辐射流体力学模拟一个高红移孤立星系，证明早期富气盘可通过Toomre不稳定性碎裂原位形成致密恒星团块，其性质与JWST观测的z>6团块相符，反馈与内部动力学调节其生长与命运。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: JWST在z>6星系中发现了极端致密的恒星团块，但其形成机制与后续演化尚不明确，需要检验其能否由早期星系盘碎裂原位形成，以及恒星反馈如何调控其性质。

Method: 对孤立星系"Ninfea_blu"（M_vir=1.5×10^10 M_⊙）进行一整套辐射流体力学模拟，从z=16演化至z=12.5，历时100 Myr，最大空间分辨率3.6 pc。

Result: 气体迅速形成转动支撑盘，峰值恒星形成率为10–15 M_⊙ yr^-1并碎裂为致密团块；Toomre不稳定区（Q_gas<1）先于恒星形成出现，首批恒星结构优先在其中形成。团块恒星质量为10^6–2×10^8 M_⊙，有效半径7–50 pc，面密度0.2–3×10^4 M_⊙ pc^-2，覆盖观测团块大部分参数空间。反馈使累积恒星质量降低30–40%，团块内滞留气体质量降低约1 dex，并压制其高质量尾部但不阻止其初始形成。团块随后迁移、相互作用、潮汐剥离并消散。团块质量函数幂律斜率为-1.88，与观测吻合；其对恒星质量与内禀UV光度的贡献从20 Myr的0.41和0.40降至100 Myr的0.13和0.10。

Conclusion: 致密富气盘的碎裂为早期宇宙中许多致密恒星系统提供了可行的起源，而反馈与内部动力学主要调控其生长与最终命运。

Abstract: (Abridged) JWST has revealed extremely compact stellar clumps in galaxies at z>6, but their formation mechanism and subsequent evolution remain uncertain. We investigate whether such systems can form in-situ through the fragmentation of an early galactic disk and how stellar feedback regulates their properties. We perform a suite of radiation-hydrodynamical simulations of an isolated galaxy ("Ninfea_blu"), with $\rm M_{vir}=1.5\times10^{10} M_\odot$, evolved for 100 Myr from z=16 to 12.5 with a maximum spatial resolution of 3.6 pc. In all cases, the gas rapidly forms a rotationally supported disk, reaches a peak star formation rate of $10-15\rm M_\odot yr^{-1}$, and fragments into dense stellar clumps. Toomre-unstable regions ($Q_{\rm gas}<1$) appear before the onset of star formation, and the first stellar structures form preferentially within these regions, supporting a Toomre-like gravitational fragmentation pathway.The clumps have stellar masses of $10^6-2\times10^8\,M_\odot$, effective radii of 7-50 pc, and surface densities of $0.2-3\times10^4\rm M_\odot pc^{-2}$, overlapping much of the parameter space occupied by observed z>6 clumps. Feedback reduces the cumulative stellar mass by 30-40%, lowers the gas mass retained within clumps by ~1 dex, and suppresses their high-mass tail without preventing their initial formation. Clumps subsequently migrate, interact, undergo tidal stripping, and disperse. The clump mass function produces a power law slope of -1.88, which aligns closely with observations. Moreover, their contributions to the stellar mass and intrinsic UV luminosity decline from 0.41 and 0.40 at 20 Myr to 0.13 and 0.10 at 100 Myr, respectively. We conclude that fragmentation of compact, gas-rich disks provides a viable origin for many of the dense stellar systems observed in the early Universe, while feedback and internal dynamics primarily regulate their growth and fate.

### [2] [N/O-enhanced chemical enrichment by massive stars: the interplay of optically-thick winds and rotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26998)
*Boyuan Liu,Michela Mapelli,Lumen Boco,Xihan Ji*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 该研究利用MESA恒星演化模型（含/不含光学厚星风），计算贫金属大质量恒星的金属产额，证明旋转大质量星的WR星风可解释高红移星系中N/O显著增强的现象。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: JWST观测发现一批高红移星系具有异常高的N/O丰度比（log(N/O)≳-1.1），部分星系还探测到WR星光谱特征。需要检验光学厚星风的旋转大质量恒星是否是这一增强的可行解释，并判断光学厚与光学薄星风模型哪个更能再现观测。

Method: 采用两套MESA单星演化模型（含与不含光学厚星风），在金属度Z⋆∼0.001–0.0045、初始自旋ω∼0–0.7的密集网格上计算∼20–100 M⊙贫金属大质量恒星的金属产额；并基于SMC的O型星观测设定初始自旋分布。

Result: 两套模型在爆发后数Myr内均能产生高达log(N/O)∼0.1的强N/O增强。含光学厚星风模型在t∼3–4 Myr因富C/O星风迅速从高N/O回落到log(N/O)∼-1.5的"正常"态，而无光学厚星风模型转变更慢更弱。含光学厚星风情景能再现RXCJ2248-ID、Sunburst Arc和MARTA 4327三个代表性高红移星系的星云C、N、O、He丰度模式，其恒星金属度和年龄与SED拟合结果一致，可解释为转变前（t≲3 Myr）、转变中（t∼3–4 Myr）和转变后（t≳5 Myr）的连贯演化序列。相反，仅光学薄星风情景喷出的C和O远少，无法再现Sunburst Arc和MARTA 4327。

Conclusion: 旋转大质量WR星的光学厚星风是解释高红移星系N/O增强及WR特征的有效机制，并能统一解释三个观测星系的丰度模式为同一演化序列的不同阶段；产额数据已公开。

Abstract: Recent JWST observations have revealed a sample of high-$z$ galaxies with highly-enhanced N/O abundance ratios, $\log\rm (N/O)\gtrsim -1.1$. Strong optically-thick winds of rotating massive stars going through the Wolf-Rayet (WR) phase provide a viable explanation for such N/O enhancements, supported by the direct spectroscopic WR signatures discovered in a few galaxies. Such strong winds are also needed to reproduce the single WR stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud (SMC). Here, we compute the metal yields of massive ($\sim 20-100\ \rm M_\odot$), metal-poor stars using two sets of \textsc{mesa} single stellar evolution models with and without optically-thick winds in a dense grid of metallicities ($Z_\star\sim 0.001-0.0045$) and initial spins ($ω\equivΩ/Ω_{\rm crit}\sim 0-0.7$). Considering an initial spin distribution based on observations of O-type stars in the SMC, we find that both sets produce strong N/O enhancements up to $\rm \log(N/O)\sim 0.1$ within a few Myr after a starburst. The models with optically-thick winds undergo a rapid transition from high N/O to the `normal' state of $\rm \log(N/O)\sim -1.5$ at $t\sim 3-4$~Myr driven by C/O-rich winds, while the transition is slower and weaker without optically-thick winds. The optically-thick-wind scenario can reproduce the nebular C, N, O, and He abundance patterns of three representative high-$z$ galaxies showing direct WR signatures: RXCJ2248-ID, Sunburst Arc, and MARTA 4327, with stellar metallicities and ages similar to those inferred from SED-fitting. This can be interpreted as a coherent evolution sequence captured before ($t\lesssim 3$~Myr), during ($t\sim 3-4$~Myr), and after ($t\gtrsim 5$~Myr) the transition. In contrast, the optically-thin-wind-only scenario ejects much less C and O via winds and cannot reproduce Sunburst Arc and MARTA 4327. The yields are available at https://zenodo.org/records/22897057.
