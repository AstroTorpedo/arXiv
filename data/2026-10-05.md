Motivation: 绿豌豆星系（GPs）是一类稀有、极端恒星形成星系，其物理性质与高红移Ly-α发射星系高度相似，可作为后者的低红移类比体。SFACT巡天提供深窄带[O III]探测，为系统搜寻此类天体提供了条件。

Method: 基于SFACT巡天中[O III]探测星系目录，结合宽带gri颜色和窄带图像导出的[O III]等值宽度进行候选体选取，得到约120个GP候选体，其光谱确认红移范围为z = 0.31-0.51，中位视星等g = 22.9；随后将其性质与已知GP样本进行对比。

Result: 获得约120个SFACT GP候选体，中位视星等g = 22.9，全部具有光谱确认红移（z = 0.31-0.51）。与已知GP样本相比，SFACT的优异深度使候选体可被探测到更低光度。

Conclusion: 该工作建立了首个SFACT绿豌豆星系候选体目录，并表明SFACT深度有利于发现低光度GP；随着光谱后续观测完成，将补充更多候选体。

Abstract: Green Pea galaxies (GPs) are a class of rare, extreme star-forming galaxies whose physical properties closely resemble high-redshift Ly-{\alpha} emitting galaxies. Their high equivalent width [O III] emission lines make them readily identifiable in surveys of emission-line galaxies like the on-going deep, narrowband Star Formation Across Cosmic Times (SFACT) survey. In this study, we use broadband gri colors and [O III] equivalent widths derived from narrowband images to select a new sample of ~120 GP candidates from a catalog of [O III]-detected galaxies identified in the SFACT survey. All SFACT GP candidates have spectroscopically confirmed redshift in the range z = 0.31-0.51. The median apparent magnitude of our sample is g = 22.9. We compare the properties of our GP candidate sample to other samples of known GPs. We find that the excellent depth of SFACT allows us to detect GP candidates to lower luminosities than these comparison samples. This is the first catalog of SFACT GP candidates; additional candidates will be added as the spectroscopic follow-up of the SFACT survey is completed.
