For every object, we measure an X-ray center at the peak of its diffuse emission, and we provide a morphological similarity search based on embeddings from the AstroCLIP foundation model, in which nearest neighbors share cool-core status in 73% of matched cluster pairs, compared with 51% by chance. Photometry of 275 point sources agrees with the Chandra Source Catalog to within 3% in the broad band. The images, along with their counts images, background models, exposure maps, and quick-look stamps, are available through a searchable web archive at this https URL . The catalog tables are archived on Zenodo (doi: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24351915 ), and all code is open source at this https URL .
